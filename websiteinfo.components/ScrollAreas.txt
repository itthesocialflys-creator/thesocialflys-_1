WHAT CAN BE INFERRED FROM STRUCTURE ALONE
----------------------------------------------
The text extraction of the page cannot see scroll-triggered CSS,
JS libraries, or Intersection Observer usage directly — none of
that is present in a reader-mode text dump. However, two structural
clues from the DOM ordering are worth flagging as hypotheses (NOT
confirmed specs):

1. Logo/partner marquee (hero section)
   The 14 partner logos (ElevenLabs, fal, Frito-Lay, RunDiffusion,
   OpenArt, ComfyUI, NVIDIA, Trapdoor, McCann, FutureBrand, FCB,
   Code and Theory, CYLNDR, Clutch) appear THREE TIMES consecutively
   in the extracted DOM order, back to back with no other content
   between repetitions. This is a strong structural signature of an
   infinite/looping marquee (the row is duplicated 2-3x in the DOM
   so a CSS transform/JS scroll loop can wrap seamlessly). This is
   an inference from repetition pattern, not a confirmed animation
   spec.

2. Numbered feature list (01-06 in "New in LTX-2.5")
   Explicit numbering (01, 02, 03...) plus one feature revealed at
   a time with accompanying video/image is consistent with a
   scroll-linked or click-linked step interface, but whether it's
   sticky-pinned, horizontally scrolling, or just a plain vertical
   stack cannot be determined from text alone.

3. Case studies section ("One foundation. Built to be put to work.")
   Five case study entries (Markov Robotics, Reactor, ComfyUI, Fal,
   Asteria), each with a "Watch the Full Video" link pointing to
   bare "#" hrefs — confirms these open a JS-driven video
   lightbox/modal rather than navigating, but not the animation
   mechanics of how that modal enters/exits.
